Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains how the game Mallow Blocks ("the game", "we", "us"), made by Stera Digital Studios, handles information. The game does not ask you for any personal information and does not require an account. To keep the game free, it shows ads provided by Google AdMob and uses Google Firebase to understand how the game is played. This policy explains what that involves.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, store or share personal information ourselves. The game never asks for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or any other personal details.

Game progress saved on your device

Your progress, such as best scores, adventure levels, coins, power-ups, unlocked block and board styles, stickers, daily streaks and settings (language, music, sound, vibration and notifications), is saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This information is not sent to us. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, your progress is deleted.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Some ads are optional: you can choose to watch a short video to continue a game, double your coins, get a hint or a power-up. Other ads may appear between games.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may automatically collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it (country or city);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • information about the ads you see and interact with, and diagnostic data such as crash information related to ads.

Game statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how players use the game so we can fix problems and make it more fun. Firebase may automatically collect information such as:

  • in-game events, for example games started and finished, levels reached and screens visited;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address;
  • an app instance ID and, where allowed, your advertising ID;
  • diagnostic information, such as crashes and app performance.

We only see this information as grouped statistics. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Information collected by Google AdMob and Firebase is processed by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Notifications

The game can send reminders on your phone, for example when a free gift is ready or to keep your daily streak. These reminders are created on your device. You can turn them off at any time in the game's settings or in your phone's settings.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where it is required, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game's privacy options.
  • Personalized ads: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (for example, Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

Children's privacy

Mallow Blocks is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data security and retention

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Information collected by Google AdMob and Firebase is kept by Google as described in its own policies. Data sent to these services is encrypted in transit.

Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information as part of providing its store and services, as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. If we add new services that handle information, we will update this policy first.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or your information, write to steradigitalstudios@gmail.com.
