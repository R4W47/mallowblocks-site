Drag, drop, pop!

Place blocks on the 8×8 board. Fill a whole row or column and it clears with a satisfying pop.

  • Drag the blocks onto the board
  • Fill a row or column to clear it
  • Clear lines back to back for big combos
  • Plan ahead: the game ends when nothing fits

Three ways to play

  • EndlessClassicNo levels, no timer. Just you, the board and your best score.
  • LevelsAdventureTravel through themed worlds, each with its own board and a chest waiting at the end.
  • Every dayDaily ChallengeA new goal every day, with the same board for everyone.
